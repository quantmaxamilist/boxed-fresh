Our story.

Real food, real people, real results — that's what Boxed Fresh has been about from day one.

Cooked by hand, made with care.

Boxed Fresh is a family-run kitchen in West Yorkshire with a simple belief: eating well shouldn't be boring, and it shouldn't eat up your week. So we do the hard part for you.

Every meal is prepped and cooked by hand by our chef — high-protein, macro-friendly and packed with flavour. No factory line, no shortcuts, just proper food in a box, delivered fresh to your door.

Whether you're training hard, working long days or just want your week sorted, we've got you covered.
