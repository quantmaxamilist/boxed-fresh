How it works.

Ordering your week of food takes a couple of minutes. Here's exactly how it goes from our kitchen to your door.

01

See the weekly menu

A fresh menu drops every week on our Instagram. Have a look at the mains, protein snack pots and breakfasts on offer.

02

Choose your plan

Pick Medium or Large portions, then a bundle from 6 up to 25 meals — whatever suits your week and your goals.

03

Pick your meals & extras

Choose your dishes, then add any breakfasts or protein snack pots. Got a dietary requirement? Just add a note and we'll accommodate it where we can.

04

Choose delivery & pay

Select the delivery option closest to you and check out securely. Card payments are taken at order.

05

Delivered fresh on Sunday

We cook everything by hand and deliver it fresh to your door every Sunday. Heat, eat, and your week is sorted.

Orders must be placed by 3pm Friday for delivery on Sunday.
